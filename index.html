About the atlas

An engineered guess, drawn to scale

Every piece here is generated in the browser from the published dimensions of the 1275 cc Cooper S — wheelbase, track, bore and stroke, disc diameter, wheel size. It is a reading of the car, not a scan of one: every pressing — bonnet, wings, doors, roof, boot lid — is lofted as one continuous surface from its own cross-sections, and the loom is modeled as routed runs. Names follow workshop-manual usage; the reference codes are this atlas's own index, not factory part numbers.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Pull the Disassemble slider and the car separates along its own assembly order — body off subframes, subframes off running gear — until every piece is laid out in one catalogue wall, sorted by system.
